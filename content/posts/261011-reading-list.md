### [Lives of the Cambridge polymath geniuses](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/qbHLGo5vu8HD3JqEM/lives-of-the-cambridge-polymath-geniuses)
  * Russell, Wittgenstein, Keynes, Turing and friends, all hanging about Cambridge.
### [Book Review: Global Economic History](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/book-review-global-economic-history)
  * Did Britain industrialise because its wages were high, so machines paid off? And why can't late developers copy the trick?
### [Was Anglo-Saxon England Really Wealthy?](https://www.markkoyama.com/p/was-anglo-saxon-england-really-wealthy)
  * The 1018 Danegeld raised £72,000, a sum not matched for two centuries. Mark Koyama argues this shows a strong state, not a rich population.
### [Age of Invention: Tudor Trade War](https://www.ageofinvention.xyz/p/age-of-invention-tudor-trade-war)
  * Henry VII banned trade with the Low Countries to punish the Habsburgs. English cloth workers ended up paying the price. The cool history of tarriffs.
