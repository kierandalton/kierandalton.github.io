### [Reasons of State: Why Didn’t Denmark Sell Greenland'](https://gwern.net/greenland)
  * A deep-dive into Denmark's 1946 refusal of a $100 million US offer for Greenland. Whether selling part of your country is wrong is a different matter.
### [How Google Maps quietly allocates survival across London’s restaurants - and how I built a dashboard to see through it](https://laurenleek.substack.com/p/how-google-maps-quietly-allocates)
  * Google Maps' ranking algorithm acts as a kind of invisible market maker, deciding which London restaurants get seen, and therefore which survive.
### [A Development Economist Returns to What He Left Behind](https://www.newyorker.com/news/letter-from-the-uk/a-development-economist-back-from-africa-considers-poverty-at-home)
  * A development economist who spent a career studying poverty in Africa comes home to Britain and finds plenty to study there too.
### [Why American ambulance rides are so expensive](https://davidoks.blog/p/why-american-ambulance-rides-are)
  * How a nice general policy ends up hurting people: America's surprise ambulance bills trace back to 1960s policy quirks that left operators structurally unprofitable.
