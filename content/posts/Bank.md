## Tier 2

### [How to spot a monopoly](https://worksinprogress.co/issue/how-to-spot-a-monopoly/)
  * Makes the case that whether more productive firms are gaining market share is a better competition test than market-concentration numbers.
### [A recent experience with ChatGPT 5.5 Pro](https://gowers.wordpress.com/2026/05/08/a-recent-experience-with-chatgpt-5-5-pro/)
  * Timothy Gowers' analysis of ChatGPT 5.5's reasoning capabilities on advanced mathematical problems.
### [AI is probably not a bubble](https://blog.peterwildeford.com/p/ai-is-probably-not-a-bubble)
  * Acknowledges the risks but argues AI firms have real revenue and product-market fit, unlike dot-com; the danger is infrastructure spend outrunning capability gains.
### [My Final EconLog Post - Econlib](https://www.econlib.org/sumner-final-econlog-post)
  * Scott Sumner's parting argument that "almost everything is downstream of integrity" — constitutions mean nothing without honest norms.
### [The Optimal Taxation of Height](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/pol.2.1.155)
  * The logical-but-absurd conclusion of standard tax theory: since height correlates with earnings, taller people should be taxed more.
### [Service costs aren't exploding anymore](https://www.noahpinion.blog/p/service-costs-arent-exploding-anymore)
  * Healthcare and education costs, the classic "cost disease" villains, have actually flattened out since around 2009.
### [Degrowth: We can't let it happen here!](https://www.noahpinion.blog/p/degrowth-we-cant-let-it-happen-here)
  * Noah Smith's pro-growth case against degrowth, in the same spirit as the Abundance/HS2 material you've already run.
### [How do we measure whether China's economy is "ahead" of America's?](https://www.noahpinion.blog/p/how-do-we-measure-whether-chinas)
  * Depends entirely on whether you use market exchange rates or purchasing power parity.
### [What will be scarce](https://aleximas.substack.com/p/what-will-be-scarce)
  * If AI makes commodities cheap, spending shifts to whatever still requires a human being involved.
### [How quickly could robots scale up](https://benjamintodd.substack.com/p/how-quickly-could-robots-scale-up)
  * From 10,000 humanoid robots a year to billions, if manufacturing costs fall the way past technologies have.
### [Against The Doomer Worldview's Bulldog](https://benthams.substack.com/p/against-the-doomer-worldview)
  * A contrarian pushback on pessimism about the future — ACX-debate shaped.
### [Yes, We Have Free Will. No, We Absolutely Do Not - Nautilus](https://nautil.us/yes-we-have-free-will-no-we-absolutely-do-not-431904/)
  * Sapolsky's hard determinism versus Kevin Mitchell's evolutionary case for real agency.
### [We are in the era of Science Slop](https://superposer.substack.com/p/we-are-in-the-era-of-science-slop)
  * AI is helping churn out papers that look rigorous but are quietly hollow.
### [The Two Big Games- Overcoming Bias](https://www.overcomingbias.com/p/the-two-big-games)
  * Robin Hanson on why markets reward betting on outcomes over currying favour with consensus.
### [Subjective time](https://scottsumner.substack.com/p/subjective-time?utm_source=post-email-title&publication_id=2934833&post_id=162417443&utm_campaign=email-post-title&isFreemail=true&r=3o9&triedRedirect=true&utm_medium=email)
  * Time feels like it speeds up with age, but your life probably contains more of "everything that matters" than it feels like.
### [The problem of underconfidence](https://scottsumner.substack.com/p/the-problem-of-underconfidence)
  * Central banks have historically underrated their own power over inflation, and paid for it.
### [Taxing Beta, Exempting Alpha](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6198558)
  * A proposal to replace the estate tax with one that only captures passive "beta" wealth growth, not genuine value creation.
### [Superintelligence is already here, today](https://www.noahpinion.blog/p/superintelligence-is-already-here)
  * Noah Smith's case that combining human-level reasoning with superhuman speed and memory already counts.
### [Your Review: School](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/your-review-school)
  * Why age-graded classrooms, for all their flaws, keep winning: they optimise for motivation, not learning efficiency.
